# RiskSentinel X — Pitches & Narratives

---

## 30-Second Pitch

Fraud systems give scores but not answers. RiskSentinel X combines XGBoost risk scoring, graph-based collusion detection, and bounded AI investigation into a single pipeline — with a deterministic policy engine that retains final decision authority. The AI investigates and recommends. Policy decides. Every step is auditable. Models detect. Graphs connect. Agents investigate. Policies decide.

---

## 60-Second Pitch

When a fraud model flags a transaction at 0.92, an analyst still needs to understand *why*. Who shares this device? Is there a coordinated ring? What does the evidence actually support?

RiskSentinel X automates that investigation. XGBoost scores transaction risk. A NetworkX graph engine discovers shared devices, IPs, and payment instruments. When evidence crosses thresholds, a bounded AI agent investigates using exactly two read-only tools — transaction history and graph context — and produces structured evidence with reason codes.

But here's the critical part: the AI never makes the final call. A deterministic policy engine maps the quantitative evidence to ALLOW, REVIEW, or BLOCK. If the AI recommends BLOCK but the hard signals are weak, policy overrides to REVIEW. No hallucination can cause a false block. Every decision is reconstructable in a complete audit trail.

Models detect. Graphs connect. Agents investigate. Policies decide.

## Demo Narrative

A normal fraud model gives a score. RiskSentinel X asks:

- **Why?** — What features drove this score?
- **Who is connected?** — Shared devices, IPs, payment instruments?
- **What evidence supports it?** — Transaction history, graph community density?
- **Should AI even investigate?** — InvestigationGate saves LLM cost for obvious cases
- **What does policy permit?** — Deterministic rules check multiple signals
- **Can we reconstruct the decision later?** — Complete audit trail

## Core Story

> "Fraud decisions should not depend on a black-box AI opinion."

RiskSentinel X combines:
- **Machine risk** — XGBoost scoring on transaction features
- **Relationship evidence** — Graph intelligence connecting entities
- **AI investigation** — Bounded agent with structured output
- **Deterministic governance** — Policy engine as sole authority
- **Complete auditability** — Every decision reconstructable

---

## Value Proposition

RiskSentinel X doesn't ask an AI to decide whom to block.

It lets models detect, graphs connect, an agent investigate, and policy make the final auditable decision.

---

## Innovation Claim

The primary innovation is **not** "we used AI." It is: AI is constrained inside an evidence-driven deterministic governance pipeline. The strongest differentiator is the separation between *recommendation* and *authority*.
