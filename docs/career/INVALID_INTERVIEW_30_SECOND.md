# RiskSentinel X - 30-Second Interview Pitch

**Interviewer:** "Tell me about RiskSentinel X."

**Answer:** 
"Many fraud systems primarily return a risk score, leaving analysts to guess *why* a transaction looks suspicious. RiskSentinel X converts that score into an actionable investigation. We use XGBoost to detect anomalies, NetworkX graph intelligence to connect related entities, and an AI agent equipped with two controlled tools to synthesize evidence. Finally, a deterministic policy engine evaluates the ML, the Graph, and the AI’s evidence to safely make an auditable ALLOW, REVIEW, or BLOCK decision. The result is a system where models detect, graphs connect, agents investigate, and policies decide, achieving a PR-AUC of 0.692 on the IEEE-CIS benchmark while mitigating LLM hallucination risks."
