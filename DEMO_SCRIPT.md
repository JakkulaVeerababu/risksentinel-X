# RiskSentinel X - 10-Minute Demo Script

With 10 minutes, you have plenty of time to show the judges how your project works. Speak slowly, sound excited, and use this easy-to-read script!

---

## 0:00 - 1:30 | The Intro & Landing Page (90 seconds)

* **Visual:** Start on the RiskSentinel X Landing Page (top of the page).
* **Script:** "Hi, I'm [Your Name], and this is RiskSentinel X. Today, online payments have a big problem. Fraud is getting faster and smarter, and old systems just can't catch it. Companies are wasting time on fake alarms while real hackers sneak through. We built RiskSentinel X to fix this."
* **Visual:** Scroll down slowly through the landing page.
* **Script:** "To stop modern fraud, we built a **4-Step AI System**:
  1. First, we use **Machine Learning** to spot strange behavior.
  2. Second, we use **Graph AI** to find hidden connections, like shared devices.
  3. Third, our **Gemini AI** works like a human detective to investigate the problem.
  4. Finally, our **Policy Engine** makes the final choice to ALLOW or BLOCK the payment."
* **Visual:** Scroll to the bottom of the page, then scroll back up and click **"Log in"**.

## 1:30 - 2:30 | The Login Page (60 seconds)

* **Visual:** Show the "Workspace Access" login screen.
* **Script:** "Security is very important. Companies can log in safely using Google, GitHub, or Email. But for this hackathon demo, we made a quick button so you can jump right in and test it."
* **Visual:** Click the **"Open workspace"** button at the bottom to enter the Dashboard.

## 2:30 - 4:00 | Dashboard Features (90 seconds)

* **Visual:** You are now on the live Dashboard. Move your mouse to show the charts and numbers.
* **Script:** "Welcome to the live RiskSentinel X dashboard. Every payment goes through our 4 AI steps in under 2 seconds. The risk team gets clear, simple alerts."
* **Visual:** Click the **"Last 24H"** dropdown and change it to **"All Time"**. Watch the numbers jump up.
* **Script:** "We can easily filter our data. If I change the view from 'Last 24 Hours' to 'All Time', you can instantly see all 1,148 payments saved in our live Supabase database."
* **Visual:** Click the **"Export CSV"** button and show the file downloading.
* **Script:** "If the risk team needs to save this data for a meeting, they can just click 'Export CSV' to download a clean report instantly."

## 4:00 - 5:30 | Live Demo 1: The Good Payment (90 seconds)

* **Visual:** Open a split screen or switch to your terminal. Hit the live Render API with the *Legitimate* payment code.
* **Script:** "Let's see it work live. I am going to send a normal, safe payment of ₹25 to our live API."
* **Visual:** Hit enter, switch back to the Dashboard. Click on the new payment that just popped up.
* **Script:** "It appears on our dashboard instantly. Because the Machine Learning score was safe, and the Graph AI found no bad connections, the Gemini Agent verified it was a good user. The system confidently **ALLOWED** the payment. A smooth experience for good customers."

## 5:30 - 7:30 | Live Demo 2: The Hacker Attack (120 seconds)

* **Visual:** Switch back to the terminal. Run the *High-Risk Fraudulent* payment code (high amount, anonymous email, VPN).
* **Script:** "Now, let's pretend to be a hacker. A bad actor tries to send a huge payment using a fake email and a hidden VPN address."
* **Visual:** Hit enter, switch back to the Dashboard. Click the red BLOCKED payment. 
* **Visual:** Click on the **Investigation AI Case** tab.
* **Script:** "Our AI caught them immediately. The Machine Learning saw the huge amount, but more importantly, the Graph AI saw they were hiding behind a VPN."

## 7:30 - 9:00 | Deep Dive: The Gemini AI (90 seconds)

* **Visual:** Stay on the Gemini Agent's output screen. Read a few bullet points out loud.
* **Script:** "This is the coolest part of the project. Instead of just giving a boring number score, our **Gemini AI** acts like a real human detective. It reads all the data and writes a clear report explaining *why* this payment is dangerous. It tells the team exactly what went wrong in plain English. This saves companies hours of manual work."

## 9:00 - 10:00 | Tech Stack & Closing (60 seconds)

* **Visual:** Go back to the main dashboard or the Landing page.
* **Script:** "RiskSentinel X is built for the real world. The website is hosted on Vercel, the Python backend runs on Render, and our database is Supabase. By combining Machine Learning, Graph Networks, and Gemini Generative AI, we stop hackers before the money even moves. Thank you for your time."

---

### Tips for Recording

1. **Take Your Time:** 10 minutes is a lot of time. Speak slowly, take a breath, and pause for a second after you click a button.
2. **Show Your Face:** Use Loom (loom.com) or OBS so the judges can see your face in the corner.
3. **Be Ready:** Have your two test codes already typed out in Notepad so you can just copy/paste them during the video.
