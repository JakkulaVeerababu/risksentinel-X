PART 3: THE 4-STEP AI PIPELINE
-------------------------------------------------------
"So, what exactly does our platform do? Instead of relying on slow, manual reviews, our platform intercepts a payment the millisecond it happens and runs it through a 4-Step AI System:

1. First, we use Machine Learning (XGBoost) to spot strange behavioral anomalies. For example, if a user is suddenly spending way too fast, or their location doesn't match their normal habits.
2. Second, we use Graph AI. This is critical. Hackers often use the same device or IP address to test hundreds of stolen cards. Our Graph AI maps these hidden connections to instantly catch coordinated fraud rings.
3. Third, our Gemini AI works like a human detective. Instead of just giving a risk score, it reads all the complex data and writes a clear, plain-English report explaining exactly why the payment is safe or dangerous.
4. Finally, our Policy Engine takes all this AI intelligence and makes the final, deterministic choice to ALLOW, REVIEW, or BLOCK the payment."
